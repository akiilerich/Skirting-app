Overlay shaker door

Backboard with stiles and rails glued on top, moulding in the panel recesses. Change any size and the drawing and cutting list update. All sizes in mm.

Door
Boards

Backboard and outer stiles and rails are cut oversize by this much on each outside edge, then the glued door is trimmed back to finished size so the edge shows no seam. Set 0 for none.

Stiles and rails

Every rail has its own width. Middle rails are numbered from the top.

Panels

Heights are the open recess between rails. The panel set to Auto takes whatever height is left.

Moulding
Stock sizes

The sizes of the boards and moulding lengths you hold. The Stock to issue section below works out how many you need. The allowance is lost to the saw between every piece.
